Yes. Many owners across Gulf Coast choose a direct sale because the house needs work, the timeline is tight, or they do not want to spend weeks on cleanup, repairs, staging, and financed-buyer uncertainty before they know what the property can really sell for.

The most common situations are inherited property, vacant homes, rental exits, older houses with deferred maintenance, probate-related sales, and owners who need a clearer closing timeline because another move is already happening.
