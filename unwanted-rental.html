Why Being a DIY Landlord in Florida is Getting Harder.

The legal landscape shifted significantly with the HB 1417 preemption, which invalidated local "Tenant Bill of Rights" in cities like Orlando and Tampa. While this created statewide uniformity, it also increased the mandatory notice period for month-to-month terminations to 30 days.

Combined with 2026 landlord insurance premiums now averaging $4,600+ in high-risk zones, many owners are finding that the "passive income" dream has become a full-time liability.

Landlord FAQ

Answers Landlords Ask Before Selling an Occupied Rental

Can I sell the house with a tenant still in it?

Yes. Florida law allows the sale of occupied property; the lease simply transfers to the new owner: us.

What if there is no written lease?

Florida law considers this a "Tenancy at Will." We can still purchase and handle the 30-day notice requirements.

Do I have to do a move-out clean-out?

No. We buy as-is, meaning you don't have to deal with the junk or repairs a tenant left behind.
