Common Pitfalls in Urgent Sales

Understanding where urgent sales can stall is essential for homeowners. Sellers need to focus on what is feasible rather than what appears ideal.

Prep Delays

Even motivated sellers can face weeks of delays due to cleanup, staging, and contractor scheduling.

Buyer Financing

A signed contract does not guarantee a close if the buyer still needs appraisal and loan approval.

Moving Deadlines

Sales tied to another move often require certainty over the potential sale price.
