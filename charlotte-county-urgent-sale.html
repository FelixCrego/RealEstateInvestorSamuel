Common Hurdles in Urgent Sales

Understanding where urgent sales typically encounter obstacles can help sellers plan effectively. It’s essential to focus on what can realistically be achieved rather than what seems ideal.

Prep Delays

Even motivated sellers can face weeks of delays due to cleanup, staging, and contractor scheduling.

Buyer Financing

A signed contract doesn’t guarantee a closing if the buyer still requires appraisal and loan approval.

Moving Deadlines

Sales tied to other moves necessitate certainty over theoretical top prices.
