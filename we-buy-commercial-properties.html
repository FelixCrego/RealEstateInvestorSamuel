Property Types

Commercial Property Types We Support

While multi-family properties are a significant focus, our expertise extends to a variety of commercial property types.

Multi-family and Apartment Buildings

We purchase duplexes, triplexes, quads, garden apartments, and larger multi-family assets where factors like turnover, vacancy, or renovations necessitate a sale.

Mixed-Use Property

We buy street-level retail spaces with apartments above, live-work buildings, and mixed-use assets where valuation is influenced by both residential and commercial income.

Retail and Strip-Center Space

We are interested in neighborhood retail, freestanding storefronts, and small centers where tenant turnover or maintenance issues prompt a change in strategy.

Office, Medical, and Flex Space

We buy owner-user buildings, office condos, medical spaces, and flex assets where capital expenditures or leasing challenges require timely decisions.

Warehouse and Light Industrial

We are open to small-bay industrial, storage, warehouse, and contractor-oriented properties where location is still favorable, but owners seek a clean exit.

Special-Use and Value-Add Assets

We evaluate hospitality, self-storage, mobile home parks, churches, and other niche assets on a case-by-case basis, prioritizing practical buyers over generic listing plans.

Why Direct Sale Matters

Common Reasons Florida Commercial Owners Seek Direct Buyers

Commercial property owners often turn to direct sales when the next hold period, lease cycle, or capital expenditure no longer seems viable.

Multi-Family Repositioning Pressure

Owners of multi-family assets may need a quicker exit due to turnover, vacancy, or renovation challenges.

Retail, Office, and Flex Uncertainty

Commercial listings can stagnate when lease rollovers or maintenance issues prevent a clean market presentation.

Entity, Partner, or Estate Decisions

Inherited portfolios or partnership dissolutions often prioritize certainty and swift execution over lengthy marketing processes.

Insurance and Carrying-Cost Concerns

In Florida, taxes, insurance, and maintenance can quickly alter the financial viability of holding a property.
