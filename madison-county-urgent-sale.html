Common Pitfalls in Urgent Sales

Understanding where urgent sales can encounter obstacles is essential. Homeowners often compare feasible options rather than theoretical best-case scenarios.

Prep Delays

Even motivated sellers can face weeks of delays due to cleanup, staging, and contractor scheduling.

Buyer Financing

A signed contract doesn’t guarantee a close if the buyer still needs appraisal and loan approval.

Moving Deadlines

When a sale is tied to another move, certainty often takes precedence over achieving the highest price.
