The Privacy Advantage

Why a Traditional Listing Can Complicate a Florida Divorce.

Florida is an "Equitable Distribution" state. Listing a home on the public MLS during a divorce can alert "vulture" buyers to your urgency, leading to low-ball offers. We provide a private, off-market sale that keeps your business out of the public eye and neighbor gossip.

In a public listing process, every price reduction, showing request, and contract delay can become another stress point between two people already navigating legal and emotional change. A private transaction reduces exposure, limits disruption, and preserves dignity while decisions are being finalized.

Divorce Property FAQ

Common Florida divorce property questions.

Do both spouses need to sign?

Yes, in Florida, marital property generally requires both signatures to transfer title.

Can we sell before the divorce is final?

Yes, many couples liquidate the home early to simplify the division of assets.

How are funds split?

The title company handles the disbursement based on agreed-upon instructions, ensuring everyone gets paid directly.
